# Decisions

Decisions made while building PSLE Quest, with the reason for each. "Owner" means decided or confirmed by the owner; "Claude" means decided by Claude while building, open to change. All dated 2026-11-24.

## Scope

| # | Decision | Why | By |
|---|---|---|---|
| S1 | Prelim papers only: 23 maths, 15 science, 14 English, 10 Chinese, 6 Higher Chinese. WA and SA2 papers are not included. | Owner chose "all prelims 2025 + 2026"; the other subjects followed the same scope. | Owner (maths), Claude (rest) |
| S2 | Family use, opened locally from `index.html`. No server, no accounts, one player. | Owner's choice. | Owner |
| S3 | Languages cover Paper 2 and the Paper 1 writing tasks where the PDF has them. Listening and oral are left out. | No audio in the PDFs; speech cannot be marked. | Claude |
| S4 | The PDFs are not in git (`*.pdf` in `.gitignore`). `library/` and `data/` are. | Owner: "exclude the pdf". Note the repo is public and `data/` holds images cropped from the papers. | Owner |

## Question library

| # | Decision | Why | By |
|---|---|---|---|
| L1 | One JSON file per paper in `library/<subject>/` is the source of truth; `data/` is generated by `tools/build_library.py`. | Hand-editable, reviewable, repeatable. | Claude |
| L2 | Every question is answered independently, then compared with the paper's key. Disagreement that survives a re-check, an unreadable key, or an unreadable question is `needs-review` and is not playable. | The keys are partly hidden by a watermark and sometimes wrong. | Owner (policy), Claude (method) |
| L3 | Where the key is demonstrably wrong, the re-checked answer is used and the question stays in play, with a note in `review.note`. Six maths questions and one science question are in this state. | A wrong key would teach a wrong answer. | Claude |
| L4 | A question whose scanned diagram or passage has a previous pupil's marks that give the answer away is held back. | The crop cannot be cleaned. | Claude |
| L5 | Explanations are written fresh, in primary-school methods (units, models, assumption), not secondary-school algebra. | Owner confirmed; keys give only terse working. | Owner |
| L6 | Question text is transcribed; diagrams are cropped from the scan at 150 dpi greyscale and stored as base64. | Sharp text, small files. | Owner (base64), Claude (rest) |
| L7 | Chinese passages are image crops, not transcribed text. Chinese questions and options are transcribed. English passages are transcribed. | A mistyped Chinese character would teach the wrong character; English transcription is reliable. | Claude |
| L8 | Questions that share a passage are separate records pointing to one shared "group". | Keeps one citation per question number and lets the mistake book hold single questions. | Claude |
| L9 | Question ids for English, Chinese and Higher Chinese are prefixed with the subject; maths and science ids are not. | The unprefixed ids clashed across subjects; maths and science ids were already in saved progress. | Claude |
| L10 | Images are split into one file per topic and loaded only when that topic is played. | The data is 62 MB; one file would open slowly. | Claude |

## Categories

| # | Decision | Why | By |
|---|---|---|---|
| C1 | Two levels: one topic per question plus one to three question-type tags. | Owner's choice. | Owner |
| C2 | Maths: 14 topics by syllabus strand, with "heuristics" only when no content topic fits. Science: 14 topics under the five MOE themes. | Matches how schools teach and revise. | Claude |
| C3 | Languages: topics are the sections of the paper (grammar, cloze, editing, comprehension, 短文填空, 改正错别字 and so on). | That is how pupils practise these papers. | Claude |
| C4 | Duplicate tags are merged at build time through `subtypeAliases` in `library/taxonomy.json`. | Many extraction runs produced near-duplicate names. | Claude |

## Marking

| # | Decision | Why | By |
|---|---|---|---|
| M1 | One attempt, then the answer and explanation are shown. | Owner confirmed. | Owner |
| M2 | Typed answers are forgiving on form (units, spaces, case, `$`, commas) and strict on value. | A right answer should not fail on formatting. | Claude |
| M3 | A fraction typed unsimplified or as a decimal gets a nudge to fix it, not a wrong mark. | It is a form error, not a value error. | Claude |
| M4 | Angles measured with a protractor accept ±1°. | Normal marking tolerance. | Claude |
| M5 | Written explanations (science Booklet B, comprehension, synthesis) are self-marked against the model answer. A typed answer identical to the model answer is marked right automatically. | Free text cannot be marked reliably offline. | Owner (self-mark), Claude (auto-match) |
| M6 | Open cloze accepts the key's word plus other words that clearly fit; the key's word is the one shown. | Several words are often correct. | Claude |
| M7 | Chinese character answers can be typed, or written on the scratch pad and self-marked when left blank. | The tablet may have no Chinese keyboard, and the exam is handwritten. | Claude |
| M8 | Compositions are not graded. Each has a checklist written for that task and a "Copy a feedback request" button for pasting into Claude or sending to a parent. | The app is offline and cannot judge writing; this keeps a parent in the loop. | Claude |

## App

| # | Decision | Why | By |
|---|---|---|---|
| A1 | One `index.html` with inline CSS and JavaScript, no libraries, no network calls; data in `data/` loaded by `<script>` tags. | Must work from a double-click on `file://`. | Owner (layout), Claude (rest) |
| A2 | Tablet first, laptop second. | Owner confirmed. | Owner |
| A3 | The canvas is scratch paper only; no handwriting recognition. | Owner confirmed. | Owner |
| A4 | Once a stylus is seen, finger touches pan instead of draw. | Palm rejection. | Claude |
| A5 | "Draw on it" copies a diagram onto the canvas. | Geometry questions need marking up. | Claude |
| A6 | Passage questions show the passage where the canvas normally is, with a Passage / Scratch pad switch, and play the passage's questions in order. | Reading needs the space more than drawing does. | Claude |
| A7 | Progress is in `localStorage` under `psle-quest-v1`, with export and import. | No server; guards against cleared browser data. | Claude |

## Gamification

| # | Decision | Why | By |
|---|---|---|---|
| G1 | XP, levels, streaks, topic medals and a mistake book. | Owner's choices. | Owner |
| G2 | 10 XP per mark for each correct part, plus a streak bonus of up to 10. Level L starts at 50 × L × (L − 1) XP. | Harder questions earn more; levels slow gradually. | Claude |
| G3 | Daily goal of 5 questions, worth 20 XP and a day streak. | Small enough to do every day. | Claude |
| G4 | Medals at 10%, 40% and 80% of a topic solved. | Early reward, long-term target. | Claude |
| G5 | A question leaves the mistake book after being answered correctly on 2 different days. | One lucky retry should not clear it. | Claude |
| G6 | Random mix favours unseen questions, mistakes and weaker topics. | Practice goes where it is needed. | Claude |
| G7 | Compositions are left out of Random mix and give a flat 30 XP. | A 36-mark task would distort levels and is too long for a mix. | Claude |

## Not built

- Phrase-bank flashcards (好词好句 and English vocabulary) drawn from the passages.
- Parent rubric scoring for compositions, with history.
- Timed mode, multiple player profiles, listening and oral.
- WA and SA2 papers.
- Testing on a real tablet with a stylus: only simulated touch and pen input has been tested.
