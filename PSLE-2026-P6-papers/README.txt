PSLE 2026 — P6 Prelim Papers

WHAT'S INSIDE — 55 papers, all 2026 P6 prelims from top Singapore
primary schools (ACS Junior, Nanyang, Tao Nan, CHIJ St Nicholas,
Catholic High, Ai Tong, Nan Hua, Red Swastika, Raffles Girls,
Henry Park, MGS, Nan Chiau, PLMGS):
- English 13, Maths 13, Science 13, Chinese 10, Higher Chinese 6
Source: sgtestpaper.com / etestpaper.com (free individual downloads).
All papers include answers.

IMPORTANT — READ THIS
These are school prelim papers written to PSLE standard. They are NOT
the official 2026 PSLE papers: SEAB does not release the actual PSLE
papers to the public, and the 2026 papers have not reached the
Ten-Year Series (TYS) books yet. The written PSLE 2026 papers were
sat 24-30 September 2026.
- The actual PSLE 2026 Science paper can be practised online at:
  https://illumitutor.com/past-year-papers/year/2026
  (search "MOE/SEAB P6 Sci PSLE 2026"; IllumiTutor also has all 157
  of its 2026 papers — prelims plus the PSLE paper — free to practise
  online with marking, but not as PDF downloads.)
- The 2025 set (168 papers, WA/SA2/prelim) is the closest free
  downloadable equivalent: ask Muse for "PSLE 2025 papers" if you
  want that zip too.

Note: free PDF downloads of 2026 papers are still thin — most sites
only carry 2025 and earlier as free PDFs, and keep 2026 prelims in
their paid online modules. This set from SG Test Paper is the full
free 2026 prelim collection available as PDFs right now.
