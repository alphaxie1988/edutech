PSLE 2025 — P6 Past Year Papers
================================
Downloaded 22 Nov 2026.

WHAT'S INSIDE
- English (29), Maths (37), Science (36), Chinese (32), Higher Chinese (21):
  2025 P6 weighted assessments (WA1/WA2/WA3) and SA2 papers from top
  Singapore primary schools (Raffles, Nanyang, Rosyth, Henry Park,
  Catholic High, Ai Tong, Nan Hua, MGS, ACS, CHIJ and others).
  Source: testpapersfree.com (free downloads).
- Prelim (13): 2025 P6 prelim papers — Maths 10, Science 2, English 1.
  Source: sgexamprep.com (free PDFs). Chinese prelims were online-only
  there, so no Chinese prelim PDF is included.

IMPORTANT — READ THIS
These are school papers written to PSLE standard. They are NOT the
official 2025 PSLE papers: SEAB does not release the actual PSLE
papers to the public. The real 2025 PSLE questions are only in the
Ten-Year Series (TYS) books sold in bookshops.
- Free final answers for the TYS papers (2012-2025, all 4 subjects):
  https://sgschoolkaki.com/exam-papers/psle
- The actual PSLE 2025 Science paper can be practised online at:
  https://illumitutor.com/past-year-papers/subject/science
  (search "MOE/SEAB P6 Sci PSLE 2025" on that page)

Answer keys: most papers include answers in the same PDF. Where a
paper has no answers, use the SG School Kaki answer keys above for
the matching TYS year, or check the school paper's source page.

Note: "P6 Science 2025 SA2 - Rosyth" was listed twice on the source
site; it appears once in this zip.
